# Code of Conduct

## Why this exists

Madhuca is a fire and smoke radar. The people who use it may be farmers, hikers, families and residents deciding what to do about a fire near them, and the people who build it come from many backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. This Code of Conduct is here so that contributing is safe, respectful and useful for all of them, and so that what we build stays honest and trustworthy.

It applies in every space the project uses: issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, commit messages, and any private channel used for project business. It also applies when someone is clearly representing the project in public.

## Our standards

**Be respectful and be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith, and remember that many contributors are students or volunteers working in a second or third language.

**Welcome everyone.** We welcome contributors of every age, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, caste, religion, language, and level of experience. Beginners are not a burden. Explain, be patient, and never make someone feel stupid for asking.

**Keep feedback about the work.** Code review should be specific, constructive and kind. Say what to change and why. Receive review in the same spirit: it is about the change, not about you.

**Be honest.** Madhuca is a safety tool, so honesty is part of conduct here. Report results as you found them. Do not fake test output, do not claim a check passed that you did not run, and do not overstate what the software can do. If you made a mistake, say so. Fixing a mistake openly is always welcome. Hiding one is not.

**Respect the people affected by fires.** Crop-residue burning is a hard problem, and many farmers who burn stubble have few affordable alternatives. Do not use this project as a platform to mock, blame or target farmers, or any community, region or language group. Discussion of smoke, pollution and policy is welcome when it stays factual, civil and relevant to the project.

**Do not misrepresent the tool.** Madhuca gives estimates from satellite data. Do not present it as an official alert system, an emergency service, or a government product, and do not present its output as certain. Do not use the project's name to imply an endorsement that does not exist.

**Stay on topic.** Keep discussion about building and improving Madhuca. Party politics, religious arguments and unrelated disputes do not belong in project spaces. Questions of map boundaries are a settled project decision, documented in the contributing guide, and are not open for debate in issues or pull requests.

**Protect privacy and security.** Do not share other people's personal information, including names, phone numbers and locations, without their permission. Never post API keys, tokens or credentials. Report security problems privately (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-a-security-problem)).

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments, in public or in private
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately provoking others
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Threats or encouragement of violence, or intimidation of any kind
- Discrimination against anyone on the grounds listed above
- Publishing someone's private information without consent ("doxxing")
- Deliberately submitting false, fabricated or misleading work, such as faked test output or fabricated data, or submitting malicious code
- Deliberately weakening the safeguards that stop the app showing a false all-clear, or hiding fires from the map
- Spam, advertising and unrelated self-promotion
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or have any concern about how the project is run, please email **mail@uncoalesced.com**.

Reports are kept confidential. We will not share the identity of the person who reported with anyone who does not need to know it in order to deal with the issue. Please include what happened, where and when, who was involved, and any links or screenshots. We will acknowledge your report, look into it fairly, and tell you what we decided. You will not be penalised for reporting in good faith.

If a report involves the maintainer who would receive it, that maintainer will step back from the decision and another maintainer will handle it.

## Enforcement

The project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and proportionate action in response to behaviour they judge inappropriate. Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, that can mean:

1. **A private or public reminder**, with an explanation of what crossed the line.
2. **A formal warning**, with clear expectations and a consequence if it continues.
3. **A temporary ban** from project spaces for a set period.
4. **A permanent ban** from all project spaces.

Maintainers may also edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will usually say why. Serious or dangerous behaviour can lead straight to a ban.

Maintainers are held to the same standards as everyone else.
