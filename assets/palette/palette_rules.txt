MADHUCA — PALETTE RULES
============================

Purpose
-------
This palette is for Madhuca: an autonomous stubble & biomass fire early-warning
radar for Punjab, Bihar, Delhi and Telangana / Andhra Pradesh.

The visual language must feel like an uncoalesced product rather than a generic
environmental dashboard. Keep the Mosaic System's restraint and one-product-accent
principle while making Madhuca's identity specific to fire monitoring, mapping,
classification and early warning.

CORE PALETTE
------------

Madhuca Green — Shade
HEX: #5D6E48
Role: Identity colour and primary green text.
Use for: Madhuca wordmark, major headings, important numerical values, active
text, high-priority labels and identity-bearing typography.

Madhuca Green — Base
HEX: #748E54
Role: Primary product accent.
Use for: selected region, primary buttons, active controls, selected map states,
key interactive affordances and product-level emphasis.

Madhuca Green — Tint
HEX: #A4B68F
Role: Quiet accent.
Use for: subtle selected backgrounds, low-emphasis highlights, hover states,
secondary map geometry and contextual emphasis.

Graphite
HEX: #333333
Role: Dark ground and standard dark ink.
Use for: top navigation, footer, dark panels, strong neutral text and structural
chrome.

Porcelain
HEX: #FDFFFC
Role: Primary light ground.
Use for: main application surfaces, cards, map-adjacent panels and clean UI
backgrounds.

Mist
HEX: #F1F3F0
Role: Secondary surface.
Use for: information panels, secondary controls, quiet status areas and
non-primary cards.

Line
HEX: #DFE0DE
Role: Structural line.
Use for: borders, dividers, separators and inactive control boundaries.

Muted Ink
HEX: #767976
Role: Secondary typography.
Use for: metadata, captions, attribution, helper text and low-priority labels.

Sandy Brown
HEX: #F19143
Role: Stubble / biomass burning semantic colour.
Use ONLY when communicating likely stubble burning or biomass-burning data.
Do not use it as generic decoration.

Watermelon
HEX: #EF2D56
Role: Wildfire / danger semantic colour.
Use ONLY when communicating likely wildfire, elevated danger or related fire
classification. Do not use it as generic decoration.

GREEN RAMP
----------

Madhuca is a single-accent product within the Mosaic System.

Shade  -> #5D6E48
Base   -> #748E54
Tint   -> #A4B68F

The shade/base/tint relationship is derived from the Mosaic product-ramp rule:
the accent is mixed 35% toward Graphite for the shade and 35% toward Porcelain
for the tint.

Do not introduce a second product accent.

SEMANTIC RULES
--------------

1. Green is product identity.
   Madhuca's identity should read green without making every piece of UI green.

2. Graphite and Porcelain establish the environment.
   The interface should primarily be neutral with green providing hierarchy.

3. Orange and red are data semantics.
   Sandy Brown means stubble / biomass burning.
   Watermelon means wildfire / danger.
   These colours should immediately carry meaning when they appear.

4. Do not use orange or red for branding, generic buttons, decoration or unrelated
   UI elements.

5. Main identity text should use Madhuca Green — Shade (#5D6E48).
   Long-form body copy should normally remain Graphite for readability.

6. Primary interactive controls use Madhuca Green — Base (#748E54).

7. Quiet interactive/background states use Madhuca Green — Tint (#A4B68F).

8. Do not recreate the old four-colour Coolors-style composition.
   The previous purple / dusty-pink / cream / green arrangement is replaced by
   this neutral + single-green-accent system.

UI APPLICATION
--------------

Top navigation:
- Graphite background.
- Madhuca Green identity treatment for the Madhuca wordmark.
- Selected region: Madhuca Green Base.
- Inactive regions: neutral typography and restrained separators.

Status / loading strip:
- Porcelain or Mist background.
- Green rule or green status indicator when appropriate.
- Avoid yellow-green warning styling.

Map legend:
- Porcelain surface.
- Line border.
- Sandy Brown marker = likely stubble burning.
- Watermelon marker = likely wildfire.
- Primary action = Madhuca Green Base.

Cards and panels:
- Porcelain for primary cards.
- Mist for secondary surfaces.
- Line for boundaries.
- Avoid large solid green cards unless the component is explicitly an active
  state or primary action.

Footer / attribution:
- Graphite ground.
- Muted light typography.
- Keep attribution visually subordinate.

MAP
---

The CARTO/OSM map should remain visually restrained.
Do not recolour the basemap into the product palette.
Madhuca's UI chrome should complement the neutral map rather than compete with it.

TYPOGRAPHY
----------

Wordmark:
JetBrains Mono, 400 / 600 / 700.

Headings and labels:
JetBrains Mono, 600, letter-spacing approximately -0.01em.

Body:
IBM Plex Sans, 400 / 500 / 600, approximately 16px with 1.6 line-height.

Eyebrow labels:
JetBrains Mono, 12px, uppercase, approximately 0.08em letter spacing,
muted ink.

VISUAL STYLE
------------

- Flat and analytical.
- Restrained.
- Technical without looking sterile.
- Environmental without becoming "eco branding".
- Map-first.
- Data-first.
- Strong hierarchy.
- Small controlled moments of green.
- No gradients.
- No neon glow.
- No glassmorphism.
- No unnecessary decorative colour.
- No independent product palette outside this specification.

MOSAIC CONSISTENCY
------------------

Madhuca follows the uncoalesced Mosaic System:
- Graphite #333333 remains the dark ground.
- Porcelain #FDFFFC remains the light ground.
- Madhuca claims Palm Leaf #748E54 as its single product accent.
- Product accent uses shade/base/tint rather than a new multi-hue product palette.
- JetBrains Mono is used for wordmarks, headings and labels.
- IBM Plex Sans is used for body copy.
- Existing Mosaic mark construction rules remain unchanged.

DATA MEANING
------------

Likely Stubble Burning:
Sandy Brown #F19143

Likely Wildfire:
Watermelon #EF2D56

Product / Active:
Madhuca Green #748E54

Product Identity Text:
Madhuca Green Shade #5D6E48

Neutral:
Graphite / Porcelain / Mist / Line / Muted Ink

DESIGN TEST
-----------

A screen is correct if:
- Madhuca is recognisably green without the entire UI becoming green.
- Green communicates product identity and interaction.
- Orange and red communicate fire classification rather than decoration.
- The neutral map remains easy to read.
- The UI looks like one coherent system rather than a Coolors palette.
- The result feels consistent with uncoalesced Mosaic while remaining specific to
  Madhuca.
